FAQ

Good to know

What is a speed ramp?
A change of playback speed inside one clip. Tempo lets you rush through the setup at up to 4× and slow the highlight down to 0.5×, without splitting the video into pieces.
Does Tempo upload my videos?
No. Tempo edits and renders your videos on your iPhone. It doesn’t need an account.
Which videos can I edit?
Videos from your Photos library or from Files.
Which iPhones does Tempo support?
Any iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
When is it coming out?
